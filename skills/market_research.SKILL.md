# Market Research Skill

## Purpose
Collect market context that sharpens the business interpretation of the dataset without replacing data evidence.

## Inputs
- Dataset profile and user objective.
- Search results with titles, URLs, snippets, and source indexes.

## Expected Outputs
- Industry overview tied to the stated decision context.
- Sourced market claims, trends, risks, opportunities, and search queries used.
- Source indexes that reports and slides can cite consistently.

## Hard Rules
- Ground each important market claim in a provided source index.
- Prefer context that changes how a business stakeholder reads the analysis.
- Separate external context from findings computed from the uploaded data.

## Failure Cases To Avoid
- Generic sector commentary unrelated to the dataset columns or user goal.
- Unsourced benchmark or trend claims.
- Treating search snippets as proof of a dataset-level result.

## Quality Checklist
- Queries reflect the objective and visible data fields.
- Claims are concise, relevant, and traceable.
- Opportunities and risks are usable by the business translator and decision maker.

## Example
For a churn objective, research current retention benchmarks and customer-risk context rather than a broad company history.
